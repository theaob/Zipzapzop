ZipZapZop

Draw one line through every square.

A calm, quick logic puzzle. Pass the numbers in order, finish on the highest one and never cross a wall. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Get it on Google Play Play in your browser

What's inside

Three new puzzles every day

Easy, Medium and Hard daily challenges, the same for everyone on the same date. Keep your streak going and share your times with friends.

The Ladder

Climb five themed worlds of 20 levels each, from Meadow 4×4 to Volcano 8×8. Fewer numbers and more walls the higher you go.

Endless mode

Random puzzles with no limit, from Easy 5×5 to Expert 8×8. Solve as many in a row as you can and chase your best run.

Learn in a minute

Four short interactive lessons teach the rules by playing. Hints and Undo help when you're stuck.

Achievements and leaderboards

Sign in to Google Play Games, if you like, to collect achievements and compare times. The game works the same without it.

Play your way

Light and dark themes, high contrast, larger text, reduced motion and an optional daily reminder. Puzzles play offline, and there's no account to make.
