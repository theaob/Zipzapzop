ZipZapZop privacy policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

ZipZapZop does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Everything the game remembers stays on your device.

What the game stores on your device

  • Your progress: daily puzzle results and times, hints used, your Ladder level and tutorial progress.
  • Your settings: the last mode and difficulty you played, accessibility options (motion, theme, contrast, text size) and whether the daily reminder is on and at what time.

This data never leaves your device through the app. Uninstalling the Android app or clearing the browser's site data deletes it. If Android's own backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include it in your device backup; that is handled by Google under your account settings, not by ZipZapZop.

Permissions (Android app)

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only if you turn on the daily reminder, to show one local notification a day, at the time you pick, reminding you to play the daily puzzles. It is skipped on days you have already solved all of them.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): used only to re-schedule that reminder after your phone restarts.

The reminder is scheduled on the device. No server sends it, and the app does not request internet access.

Sharing results

The Share button opens your device's share sheet with a short text such as your solve time. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app to share with, and what happens next is governed by that app's own policy.

Web version

The browser version loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers to download them (see Google's privacy policy). The Android app bundles its fonts and makes no such request.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.
